How The Tool Works

Rental Deal Analyzer helps real estate investors quickly evaluate rental properties before making a purchase decision.

  • Open the Calculator: Click “Open Free Calculator” to launch the tool.
  • Enter Deal Inputs: Fill in purchase price, rent, expenses, financing, and assumptions.
  • Analyze the Deal: Click “Analyze Deal” to instantly see deal results.
  • Review Results: View Deal Results, Expense Breakdown, and Cash Required at Closing.
  • Switch View Mode: Toggle between Monthly and Annual analysis.
  • Download & Share: Export results as Excel or PDF for partners, lenders, or record keeping.
