How Rental Deal Analyzer Works

Rental Deal Analyzer is a free online rental property calculator designed for beginner and experienced real estate investors to quickly evaluate rental deals.

  1. 🚀 Open the Calculator
    Click Open Free Calculator to launch the Streamlit-based rental deal analyzer.
  2. 🧮 Enter Deal Inputs
    Provide purchase price, rehab cost, monthly rent, operating expenses, vacancy rate, management fee, loan terms, and down payment.
  3. 📊 Analyze the Deal
    Click Analyze Deal to instantly calculate:
    • Cash Flow
    • Net Operating Income (NOI)
    • Cap Rate
    • Cash-on-Cash Return
    • Equity Percentage
    • Rental Deal Score (0–100)
  4. 🔄 Switch View Mode
    Toggle between Annual and Monthly views to understand both short-term and long-term performance.
  5. ⬇️ Download & Share
    Download your rental analysis as Excel or PDF to share with partners, lenders, or for record keeping.
